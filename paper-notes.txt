PAPER: Development and prospective validation of a machine learning model to predict mortality 
in cirrhosis with esophageal variceal bleeding
Published: World J Hepatol 2026; 18(2): 111099
DOI: 10.4254/wjh.v18.i2.111099
Authors: Rech MM, Corso LL, Dal Bó EF, Ferraza AD, Tomé F, Terres AZ, Balbinot RS, Balbinot RA, Balbinot SS, Soldera J

KEY FINDINGS:
- Retrospective cohort: 94 patients (2010-2016), Prospective validation: 24 patients (2017-2018)
- 36 clinical, laboratory, and imaging variables included
- Random forest model achieved highest AUC: 0.91 (95% CI: 0.85-0.96) internal validation
- Prospective validation: AUC 0.88 (95% CI: 0.80-0.94)
- Calibration: Brier score 0.12 (excellent)
- Five-fold cross-validation for calibration
- Model deployed at: https://huggingface.co/spaces/mmrech/evb-br
- Frontend: Gradio 5.3, Backend: Python 3 with scikit-learn

36 VARIABLES (from paper):
Age, sex, cause of cirrhosis, use of omeprazole, hepatorenal syndrome, use of spironolactone, 
use of furosemide, previous use of non-selective beta-blockers, hemodialysis, portal vein thrombosis, 
ascites, hepatocellular carcinoma, encephalopathy, serum albumin, total bilirubin, direct bilirubin, 
prothrombin time, INR, creatinine, platelet, AST, ALT, leucocytes, hemoglobin, hematocrit, 
sodium, potassium, time-to-endoscopy, size of varices, red wall marks, rupture points, 
active bleeding, therapy used for treatment, terlipressin doses, race, rebleeding

TRADITIONAL SCORES COMPARED:
- MELD (AUC 0.752, 95%CI: 0.654-0.850)
- MELD-Na (AUC 0.762, 95%CI: 0.665-0.859)
- Child-Pugh (AUC 0.764, 95%CI: 0.667-0.861) - best traditional
- Glasgow-Blatchford (AUC 0.635, 95%CI: 0.527-0.743) - worst

SHAP FEATURE IMPORTANCE (from paper):
Most influential: race, total bilirubin, INR, albumin, creatinine, hepatorenal syndrome
Secondary: medication use (beta-blockers, spironolactone, furosemide), clinical parameters 
(ascites, hepatocellular carcinoma)

MODEL DETAILS:
- CalibratedClassifierCV with 5-fold cross-validation
- Random Forest with calibration (isotonic)
- Preprocessing: KNN imputation (k=10), one-hot encoding, StandardScaler
- No feature selection - all 36 variables used
- Class imbalance addressed via stratified sampling (51.1% mortality)
